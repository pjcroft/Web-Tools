From long PDF to short form

1. Choose a PDF. Existing fields, text cues, printed boxes, and optional English OCR help find relevant pages.

2. Review the suggested pages. Show all pages to include any missed matches.

3. Fill the fields. Use Adjust layout to move, resize, rename, or remove fields. Add anything the scan missed.

4. Export only selected pages as a fillable or completed PDF.

Detection is heuristic and needs review. Scans with handwriting, other languages, or unusual layouts may need manual fields. Original printed text is preserved as the page background; the overlaid form fields are editable. Unsaved work is lost when this tab closes.
